PayPal Finance Setup Brief

Helanka Vacations — Finance Team — June 2026

Part A: One-Time Bank & PayPal Setup

What the finance team needs to do before we go live

1
Open a Sampath Bank Foreign Currency (USD) Account

Contact your Sampath branch relationship manager. Request a USD-denominated foreign currency account. You will need the business registration documents. Collect and save:

  • USD account number
  • SWIFT code: BSAMLKLX
  • Branch name and full branch address
  • Account holder name (must match your PayPal business name exactly)
2
Set Up a PayPal Business Account

Go to paypal.com and register a Business account under the Helanka Vacations business name. Complete identity verification (KYC). Make sure the account is registered in Sri Lanka.

3
Link the Sampath USD Account to PayPal

In PayPal, go to Wallet → Link a bank account. Enter the Sampath USD account details (account number, SWIFT code, branch). PayPal may take 2–3 days to verify the link with small test deposits.

4
Test a Small Withdrawal

Before handling real client payments, withdraw a small amount (e.g. $10) from PayPal to the Sampath USD account. Confirm the full process works end-to-end and note how many days it takes. This is critical — do not skip this.

5
Confirm Environment Variables Are Set to Live

Coordinate with the development team to switch PAYPAL_MODE from sandbox to live in the website's environment variables. Do a test transaction on the live site to verify the payment appears in the PayPal dashboard and the booking status updates in the system.

If PayPal doesn't allow direct withdrawal to Sampath: Some Sri Lankan accounts may not have bank withdrawal enabled. If this is the case, the fallback is to use Payoneer as an intermediary (PayPal → Payoneer → Sampath USD). This adds ~2% cost and 3–5 extra days. Contact PayPal support first to check eligibility before setting up Payoneer.

Part B: What Happens When a Client Pays

The payment flow from client to your bank account

1. Client pays deposit via PayPal on the website ↓ 2. PayPal processes the payment and deducts fees (~5% + $0.49) ↓ 3. Net amount lands in Helanka's PayPal USD balance ↓ 4. Website automatically records the payment and confirms the booking ↓ 5. Finance team transfers the PayPal balance to Sampath USD account ↓ 6. Funds arrive in Sampath USD account in 3–5 business days (free from PayPal) ↓ 7. Sampath deducts a small incoming wire fee (~$2–$5)
Key rule: Always withdraw to the Sampath USD account, not an LKR account. Converting currency inside PayPal costs an extra 3–4%. If you need LKR, convert at Sampath — the bank rate is better.

Part C: How to Withdraw from PayPal

Steps to transfer money from PayPal to Sampath

1
Log in to PayPal

Go to paypal.com and sign in with the business account.

2
Go to Wallet → USD Balance

Click on your USD balance to see the available funds.

3
Click "Transfer to Bank"

Select the linked Sampath USD account as the destination.

4
Enter the Amount and Confirm

Choose "Standard transfer" (free, 3–5 business days). Instant transfer is not available for international bank accounts.

Part E: Monthly Finance Routine

What to do each month

1
Download PayPal Monthly Statement

PayPal → Activity → Statements → Monthly (CSV or PDF).

2
Reconcile Against Client Invoices

Match each PayPal transaction to a booking. The amount charged to the client is your revenue figure. The amount received in PayPal is your bank receipt.

3
Record PayPal Fees as an Expense

The difference between what the client paid and what you received is the "payment processing fee" expense.

4
Transfer Accumulated Balance to Sampath

Follow the withdrawal steps in Part C. Note the Sampath incoming wire fee for your records.

Part F: Important Warnings

Refunds: If you refund a client via PayPal, PayPal does NOT refund the transaction fee back to you. You lose the ~5% even on cancelled bookings. Factor this into your cancellation policy.
Currency conversion: Never convert USD to LKR inside PayPal. The PayPal conversion rate costs an additional 3–4%. Always withdraw as USD to Sampath, then convert at the bank if needed.
Balance payments: The deposit is the only payment handled through PayPal/the website. The remaining balance is collected separately via bank transfer — the client contacts you directly for this.
