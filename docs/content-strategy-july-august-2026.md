## 2. Seasonal Strategy: July–August

July–August is **southwest monsoon** in Sri Lanka. The east coast is dry and sunny — this is the content pivot.

### Lead Destinations (promote heavily)

| Destination | Content Angle | Key Visuals |
|-------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Arugam Bay** | Sri Lanka's surf capital meets boutique luxury. International surf competitions in July. | Surf breaks at dawn, boutique hotel pools, street food stalls |
| **Trincomalee / Pigeon Island** | Snorkeling, reef sharks, dolphins, whale watching. "Undiscovered luxury" angle. | Underwater shots, boat trips, Nilaveli beach |
| **Passikudah** | Flat turquoise water, still-water luxury aesthetics. Most photogenic for carousels. | Infinity pools, kayak flat-lays, sunrise swims |
| **Sigiriya** | Ancient rock fortress + east coast combo. Justify a 10–14 day trip. | Summit views, lion's paw entrance, drone aerials |
| **Dambulla / Polonnaruwa** | Cultural Triangle is dry year-round. Pair with east coast for "culture + beach" itinerary. | Cave temples, ancient ruins, local village life |

### Do NOT Promote (monsoon-affected)

Unawatuna, Mirissa, Hikkaduwa, Galle beaches, Bentota, Nuwara Eliya hill country, Colombo coastal areas.

> **Exception:** Galle Fort (the town, not the beach) and Kandy can appear in cultural context — frame as "rainy season charm" only if authentic.

## 5. Organic Growth Tactics

### Engagement Loop (Daily, 15 min)
- Respond to every comment within 2 hours
- Comment on 10–15 travel posts from accounts with 2–5K followers (authentic engagement, not competitor replies)
- Like and reply to tagged/mentioned content

### Weekly Activations
- **Traveler Spotlight (Monday):** Repost real guest content, tag them, ask community questions
- **Caption This Contest (Wednesday):** User votes on captions, winner gets featured
- **Ask Jenna Live (Friday):** Instagram Live Q&A, 30 min, destination deep-dives

### Collaboration Strategy
- Partner with micro-influencers (10K–100K followers) in travel/wellness niches
- Offer Helanka trip packages in exchange for authentic UGC
- Cross-promote with Sri Lankan hospitality brands (boutique hotels, local restaurants)

### Community Building
- Join and contribute to travel planning Facebook groups (Sri Lanka, luxury travel, honeymoon planning)
- Share destination guides from helanka.co/blog (non-promotional, value-first)
- Reddit: r/travel, r/solotravel, r/srilanka — share guides, link to blog

## 10. Risk: AI Influencer Trust

Research shows virtual influencers average 5.67% engagement (vs 1.89% human), but 46% of consumers report discomfort with AI faces. Mitigation:

1. **Full transparency** — bio disclosure, watermarks, behind-the-scenes content showing the AI process
2. **Mix with real UGC** — cross-promote real guest content alongside Jenna's posts (target 30% real guest content)
3. **Community-first** — Jenna responds to comments, runs polls, asks questions — not just broadcasting
4. **Value over vanity** — every post teaches, inspires, or helps plan. If it's just pretty, skip it.
